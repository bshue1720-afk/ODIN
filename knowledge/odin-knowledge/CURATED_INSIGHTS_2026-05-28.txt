## CATEGORY C — REAL ESTATE (GENERAL)
----------------------------------------------------------------------

### "Real Estate Is Just a Symptom of Human Problems" (Rick Ginn core teaching)
Source: Skool — Step by Step Closing Guide, Qualifying Sellers, Objections
Every distressed property has a story behind it. Vacant = couldn't pay mortgage, foreclosure,
probate, eviction. Tax delinquent = financial distress. The property is the symptom; the human
problem is the root. Whoever gets to the truth fastest wins the deal.

### Certainty Is the Product
Source: Skool — Handling Objections
Wholesalers sell certainty. Cash = no financing contingency, no inspection repair demands, no
90-120 day MLS wait. When sellers push back on price, pivot to certainty: "I can't give you the
highest offer, but I can give you the best offer — cash, 30 days, as-is, no surprises."
Average house costs 12% to sell on MLS (6% commission + closing costs + repair requests +
mortgage payments during listing). A wholesale offer at 80% of ARV often nets MORE than MLS.

### Inspection Period = "Get Out of Jail Free"
Source: Skool — What is Wholesaling
30-45 day inspection period in every purchase contract allows exit for any reason, no questions
asked. This is legal precedent (realtors use it routinely). Never tell a seller you MUST buy —
always: "subject to inspection period." If no buyer found, cancel within inspection window.

### Virtual Wholesaling Works
Source: Skool — Virtual Wholesaling Lead Walkthrough, Boots on the Ground
Boots on the ground: local contact walks the property on video call. Can be another wholesaler,
handyman, or anyone local. Works for any market virtually. Memphis TN is a strong market — active.

### MLS Deals vs. Wholesale Deals
99% of MLS properties sell at retail. The 1% exception: confused sellers who belong in wholesale.
Never compete on MLS. Off-market only. Cash buyers pay premium for off-market because they save
$30-50K vs. MLS retail prices (Zach's example: cash buyer saves $40K, happily pays $20K finder fee).

### THE MILLION DOLLAR COLD CALLING SCRIPT
Source: Skool — Cold Calling (Zach)
Two-line script — do NOT overcomplicate it:
  Line 1: "Is this the owner of [123 Main Street]?"
           (Throws them off — they think it's something real/important. Gets them to YES.)
  Line 2: "Hey, this is [name]. I had a quick question — are you interested in selling that property?"
Why it works: Opens with address (personal, specific) → breaks "no mindset" → puts them in yes mode.
Use seller's name as many times as possible without being creepy — most dopamine-producing word.
Cold calling is NOT acquisitions. It is ONLY to find interested sellers → put in CRM → close later.

### MCTP QUALIFYING FRAMEWORK (confirmed by both Zach and Rick)
Source: Skool — Qualifying Sellers, Cold Calling, Step by Step Closing Guide
FOUR PILLARS (in order — do NOT skip to Price):
  M — Motivation: "What is your reason for selling?" → ZIP YOUR LIP. Get the STORY.
                  Real estate = symptom of human problem. Vacant/foreclosure/probate/job loss.
                  If you don't get the story, you're a transactional wholesaler who loses on price.
  C — Condition:  "Tell me about the current state of the house." Open-ended. Let THEM talk.
                  80/20 rule: you talk 20%, they talk 80%. More they talk = more data = better deal.
  T — Timeframe:  "In your ideal scenario, when are you looking to sell?"
                  If vague, use: "If I bought your house — where would you be relocating to?"
                  Their FIRST response = their truth. Bucket timeframes:
                    <30 days = drop everything, close now
                    30-90 days = NORMAL, most deals here
                    90-180 days = lighter follow-up, once/month
                    180+ days = long-term drip, move to CRM automation
                  70% of deals come from FOLLOW-UP. Not the first contact.
  P — Price:      "What do you NEED to get out from under this property?" (NOT "asking price")
                  Never: "What are you asking?" "What do you want?" (triggers retail mindset)
                  If unrealistic: "How'd you come up with that number?" then zip lip.
                  Do MCT FIRST. Price only makes sense in context of the story.

### ODN AVATAR — Your Ideal Seller
Source: Skool — Step by Step Closing Guide (Rick)
OFF MARKET | DIRECT TO SELLER | NEEDS TO SELL
All three must be true. NOT: on MLS / through agent / just wants to sell.
"Busy means broke" — chasing want-to-sell leads fills your calendar and empties your bank account.

### OFFER FORMULA (MAO / LAO)
Source: Skool — How to Calculate Offer Price
MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer) = (ARV x 0.70) - Repairs
LAO (Least Allowable Offer) = 70% of MAO (your FIRST offer, should be uncomfortable)
Example: ARV $430K, Repairs $50K → MAO = $251K → LAO = ~$175K
Lock up at LAO if possible. Assignment fee = difference between lock-up price and what buyer pays.
Use Zach's free AI calculator (freewholesaling.com) or XLeads built-in MAO calculator.

### SIX SELLER OBJECTIONS + CURES (Rick Ginn — masterclass)
Source: Skool — Handling Common Objections
OBJECTION 1: "What's your offer?" (before qualifying)
  Underlying: controlling personality, wants to use your number against you
  Cure: "For me to give you the best offer, I have to ask a few questions. May I?"
        Car analogy: "Would you buy a car without test driving it? I can't offer without seeing it."
  NEVER: give a number before MCTP. You will lose on price every time.

OBJECTION 2: "I need to talk to so-and-so" (spouse/brother/partner)
  Underlying: you don't have the decision maker in front of you
  Cure: Conditioning agreement UP FRONT: "Will all decision makers be available?"
  If they said yes, remind them: "You told me upfront you were the sole decision maker."
  NEVER present to a non-decision maker. They will only relay the price, kill the deal.
  "Telephone game" — by the time it gets to the decision maker, only the bad parts survive.

OBJECTION 3: "I need to think about it"
  Underlying: lack of motivation OR lack of trust in you (most common = trust)
  Cure: Go back to conditioning agreement. Rebuild rapport. "What's concerning you most?"
  "I think about it" = stall. Must progress every prospect. If not moving forward, they're
  pulling info from you without giving any back. Hail Mary or move on.

OBJECTION 4: "I need my lawyer to review it"
  Underlying: testing you (9/10 times they don't have a lawyer). Low rapport.
  Cure: EMBRACE IT. "That's a great idea." Ask: "Who's your lawyer? Can I send it directly?"
  → Most go silent. No lawyer exists.
  If real lawyer: use state-approved contract. Price-anchor with seller FIRST before sending.
  "We're agreed on $200K, correct? No matter what the lawyer says about verbiage, we're good on price?"
  Lawyers look at price and try to inflate. Anchor it before they see the contract.
  What we do is legal and above board — don't be scared of lawyers. Be transparent.

OBJECTION 5: "I got a higher offer"
  Underlying: fishing for more money, or offer isn't real
  Cure: "Why didn't you take it?" → Most higher offers are hearsay (Zillow, friend said, realtor)
  Response: "I'm not going to give you the highest offer. I'm going to give you the best offer."
  Go back to WHY they called you originally (notes in CRM). Remind them of the root problem.
  Sell certainty: cash, as-is, 30 days, no repairs, no contingencies.
  Neighbor offer = almost never real. Ask for proof of funds. They'll disappear.

OBJECTION 6: "I'm considering listing with a realtor"
  Underlying: uncertainty about you, possibly didn't qualify motivation properly
  Cure: Use their timeframe against it. "Average DOM in this market is 72 days — that's 90-120 days
  total from listing to close. You said you wanted this done in 45 days. Does that work?"
  Remind: 12% cost to sell via MLS (6% commish + closing costs + repairs + carrying costs).
  If they truly want full price: let them list, get a 25-33% marketing referral fee.
  "I can't convince people to do what they don't want to do. I can only help people who need to sell."

### COLD CALLING NUMBERS (KPIs to track)
Source: Skool — Cold Calling
  Contact rate: ~25% (1 in 4 answer). 75% don't pick up.
  Lead rate: ~1 per 100 contacts (want to sell, cash offer interested)
  "Second chance leads" — text the 75% who don't answer → 20% text response rate →
    adds 15% more contacts → total reach goes from 25% to ~40%
  Best calling times: 2pm - 7pm
  Minimum commitment: 10 hours/week (2h/day, 5 days)
  Dialer rule: 3 phone numbers per 10 hours of calling per week (rotate when marked spam)
  Contact rate drops to 3-4%? You're spam-likely. Call your own number to check.

### CONDITIONING AGREEMENT (pre-appointment framing)
Source: Skool — Handling Objections, Step by Step Closing Guide, Qualifying Sellers
Before every appointment/conversation, establish upfront:
  "We're going to spend some time. We both agree to respect each other. At the end, you'll give me
  a yes or no answer. Either way, I'll help you regardless."
  Also: "Will all decision makers be available?" → get YES on record.
  This pre-empts 3 of the 6 objections (think about it, decision maker, stalling).
  Without conditioning agreement, sellers stay in "friend zone" — indefinite stalling.

### DISPO (SELLING YOUR CONTRACT)
Source: Skool — How to Sell Your Wholesaling Deals
  Blast deal to buyers list immediately on locking up contract.
  Include: address, ARV, asking price, repair estimate, photos
  Collect earnest money deposit from buyer before removing from market.
  Use JV partner (Zach's team) if stuck — split 50/50. Better to make 15K than miss the deal.
  Title company handles closing — doesn't cost you anything.

### TITLE COMPANY
Source: Skool — Title Companies, Step by Step Closing Guide
  Use wholesaler-friendly title companies only (not all will handle assignments).
  Title company = neutral 3rd party. Handles all money flow. Closes the deal.
  Your assignment fee paid AT CLOSING — title company sends it directly to you.
  Ask title company to review contract instead of seller's lawyer — they'll do it for free.

### CONTRACTS + LEGAL
Source: Skool — Filling Out the Contract, How Taxes & LLC's Work
  Two documents: Purchase Sale Agreement (with seller) + Assignment of Contract (to cash buyer)
  Inspection period = your exit clause. 30-45 days. Legal to back out for any reason.
  Use state-approved real estate contract (not wholesale-specific) for lawyer-concerned sellers.
  Key clause: make sure contract is ASSIGNABLE (some standard contracts remove this — add it back).
  E-sign: PandaDoc (free, not legally certified) or XLeads/DocuSign (legally certified, IP tracked).
  XLeads e-sign is free with subscription. DocuSign is $30-40/month.

### JV SYSTEM
Source: Skool — JV System
  Joint venture = bring in a partner to help close or dispo. Split deal 50/50.
  Zach's team does JVs — you find the deal, they help close/dispo, 50/50 split.

  Never be too proud to JV. Half a deal > zero deal.
