Partner & Referral Channels

Someone else does the outreach — you just fulfill. Highest close rate of any channel.
Dan Martell (from transcripts) "One strategic partner generates 10x more pipeline than one customer." The partner model is the only channel that truly runs without you doing the outreach. One relationship = dozens of warm referrals with zero ad spend.

How to Structure a Referral Partner Agreement

Commission Rate

5–15% of first contract value, OR $500–1,000 flat per closed deal. For recurring services: 5–10% monthly for the life of the client.

Qualifying Criteria

Define what counts as a referral: must be a decision-maker, must have confirmed budget, must have spoken with your partner first (not just a cold name drop).

Payment Terms

Net-30 after client payment clears. Track with unique referral links (Rewardful, $49/mo) or a simple Google Sheet + Stripe manual payment.

Escalating Bonuses

Base commission + milestone bonuses at 5, 10, 20 referrals. Creates gamification. Top referrers become advocates who actively promote vs. occasionally mention.

How to Pitch a Referral Partner (3 Sentences)

Template "We both serve [specific client type]. You handle [X], I handle [Y] — they often need both. I'll pay you [15–20%] for every referral that closes, monthly for the life of the client. Here's a one-pager you can forward when the topic comes up."

The one-pager does the selling for them. Keep their ask under 30 seconds — if they have to explain your service, it won't happen consistently.

Industry Association Partnerships

Higher leverage but slower. One presentation to an association's membership can generate 5–50 warm leads. Pitch the executive director with a member benefit (free webinar, resource guide, member discount) rather than asking for referrals. You present free, offer 10% of client revenue back to the association, and get warm intros to their entire roster.

What's Working vs. What's Dead in 2026

✓ What's Working

  • Hyper-targeted cold email with Clay-generated first lines that reference a specific event, job change, or post. 2–5x reply rate vs. generic.
  • SMS automation — 45% response rate. Dramatically underused in B2B. Missed call text-back is the single highest-ROI automation for any service business.
  • LinkedIn content (not DM spam) — process videos and before/after automation results. Undersaturated. B2B purchase intent is highest on LinkedIn.
  • Google Business Profile — fully free, one business reports 40%+ of leads from GBP alone. Reviews compound automatically once you set up the request trigger.
  • Upwork for AI services — AI-related work grew 60% in 2025. Clients actively searching. Well-optimized profiles get inbound without any proposals.
  • Partner/referral — highest close rate (25–50%), zero ongoing cost, scales with relationship depth.

✗ What's Saturated or Dead

  • Generic cold email at scale — sub-2% reply rates, Apple Mail privacy inflating open rates, increasing spam filter sophistication. Only works with tight targeting + real personalization.
  • LinkedIn mass-connect spam — acceptance below 15% for generic outreach. LinkedIn actively restricting automation. Artisan was banned for two weeks in late 2025.
  • Bark.com at face value — majority of independent reviews report poor lead quality. Test with small credit pack first.
  • AI SDR tools at launch — $900–$5,000+/month tools need a sales cycle and close rate you haven't validated yet. Cost per reply on 11x: ~$2,500.
  • TikTok for direct B2B conversion — great for awareness, weak for converting B2B buyers. Better to build awareness on TikTok and convert on LinkedIn or email.
