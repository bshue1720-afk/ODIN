  SECTION 1 — WHAT IS ODIN?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ODIN is a self-operating business intelligence and automation system.
It is a general-purpose business operating system — NOT a real estate
tool, not an industry-specific platform, not a CRM. Real estate is one
business that runs inside ODIN. Multiple businesses (called "spokes")
can run simultaneously through the same infrastructure.

The core idea: one person (Brock) should be able to run multiple
revenue-generating businesses without being the bottleneck. ODIN handles
the repetitive, time-sensitive, and data-heavy work. Brock handles
relationships, high-stakes decisions, and defining what to build next.

ODIN receives commands through Slack. It listens to a workspace, parses
plain-English commands, executes actions, and posts results back — all
without opening a browser or leaving the phone. Discord provides a
secondary voice channel where a daily briefing is read aloud.

What ODIN does right now:
  - Scores and tags every inbound lead automatically (no manual triage)
  - Fires follow-up SMS on scheduled dates without reminders
  - Tracks KPIs for every business in real time with traffic lights
  - Surfaces pending decisions in a structured queue and auto-executes
    them after 48 hours if not reviewed
  - Delegates tasks to the right person (human or AI) and escalates
    overdue items automatically
  - Tracks revenue and expenses per business (spoke)
  - Onboards new buyers and clients through automated pipeline stages
  - Delivers a daily briefing every morning at 6am (Slack + Discord voice)
  - Runs 13 background jobs on a schedule with no manual triggering

What ODIN is NOT:
  - Not a real estate wholesaling tool exclusively
  - Not dependent on any single business type
  - Not a tool Brock has to babysit — it runs on its own

  SECTION 2 — THE GOAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The goal is full APEX across every business ODIN manages.

APEX means a business runs itself. Revenue is generated, leads are worked,
follow-ups fire, KPIs are tracked, and decisions are surfaced — without
the owner needing to check in every day.

The secondary goal is speed of launch. When Brock wants to start a new
business, ODIN should be able to absorb it as a new "spoke" and have
automation running within hours. Lead capture, follow-ups, revenue
tracking, briefings, and task delegation should all be available
immediately for any new spoke.

Brock's time should go toward:
  → Reviewing exceptions flagged by ODIN
  → Making decisions ODIN cannot make alone
  → Building relationships and closing deals
  → Deciding what the next spoke should be

Everything else should run without him.

  SECTION 3 — WHAT IS APEX?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

APEX is a 10-dimension framework for measuring business autonomy. Each
dimension is scored 0–100%. A business at 100% APEX across all dimensions
does not require the owner's daily involvement to operate and grow.

ODIN's job is to push every spoke toward 100% APEX on every dimension.

  DIMENSION 1: LEAD QUALIFICATION
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does every inbound lead get scored and routed automatically?
  100% = zero manual triage. Score, tag, and route happens before the
  owner ever sees the lead. Hot leads trigger approval queue immediately.

  DIMENSION 2: OFFER GENERATION
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Can a complete offer package be produced from a single command?
  100% = ARV estimate, rehab cost range, max allowable offer, buyer demand
  score, and deal verdict generated automatically from an address alone.

  DIMENSION 3: SMS OUTBOUND
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Can bulk and targeted SMS campaigns launch from a single command?
  100% = blast fires, contacts enroll in sequences, opt-outs handled,
  deliverability monitored — all from Slack with no manual setup.

  DIMENSION 4: FOLLOW-UP AUTO
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Do follow-up messages fire on schedule without reminders or manual sends?
  100% = Brock sets a date and a message once. ODIN fires it at the right
  time and logs the action. The owner never needs to remember.

  DIMENSION 5: KPI VISIBILITY
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does ODIN know the real-time health of every business metric?
  100% = live traffic lights (green/yellow/red) for every KPI across every
  spoke. Auto-alert when anything drops below target. No spreadsheets.

  DIMENSION 6: TEAM DELEGATION
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does ODIN assign, track, escalate, and execute tasks automatically?
  100% = tasks have owners and deadlines. ODIN pings on overdue. AI-tagged
  tasks run themselves via Haiku. Brock only sees escalations.

  DIMENSION 7: REVENUE TRACKING
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does ODIN know the MTD profit/loss for every spoke at any moment?
  100% = every income and expense event logged automatically or via one
  command. Spoke targets set. Morning briefing includes revenue snapshot.

  DIMENSION 8: DECISION QUEUE
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Are pending decisions surfaced proactively with options and recommendations?
  100% = every real decision is logged in ODIN with 1-3-1 format (one
  situation, three options, one recommendation). Approved or auto-executed
  at 48 hours. Nothing stalls silently.

  DIMENSION 9: VOICE BRIEFING
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does ODIN deliver a spoken daily briefing without requiring a screen?
  100% = a phone call at 6am CT reads the briefing aloud. Eyes-free.
  No app, no screen, no notification to dismiss.

  DIMENSION 10: BUYER/CLIENT ONBOARDING
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Does ODIN automatically move new buyers and clients through an onboarding
  pipeline from first contact to active status?
  100% = new contact enters pipeline, gets welcome message, progresses
  through qualification steps, lands in 'active' with no manual work.

  SECTION 9 — RULES FOR THIS CODEBASE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Follow these when writing or modifying any ODIN code:

  1. APEX-first, spokes-second.
     Build ODIN as a general OS. Do not add real-estate-specific or
     Valdr-Ops-specific logic to the core system. All business logic
     lives in spoke configuration, not in core routes or heartbeat jobs.

  2. Single worker — always.
     Procfile must use --workers 1. APScheduler (the job scheduler) does
     not work across multiple workers. This is why morning briefings
     previously never fired. Do not change this.

  3. Architecture rules:
     - Every DB table must have a user_id foreign key
     - Every API endpoint must call check_permission_role() before executing
     - Every agent action must log to the agent_actions table
     - API keys live in Railway environment variables — never hardcoded

  4. Eddie is never auto-assigned.
     The task auto-assignment rules only route to 'brock' or 'odin'.
     Eddie self-assigns tasks. He never receives auto-assigned work.

  5. Maya edits happen in XLeads only.
     The Voice AI agent is configured through the XLeads builder UI.
     There is a 'maya update' Slack command — ignore it. It does not
     reliably update the agent. Use the UI.

  6. 8,655 buyers are in the database.
     Any buyer automation (welcome SMS, onboarding sequences) must check
     that xleads_contact_id IS NOT NULL before firing. These buyers were
     bulk-imported and must not receive unintentional mass messages.

  7. STATUS.txt is ground truth for session state.
     File: ODIN/System/STATUS.txt
     Read it at the start of every session. It reflects what is built,
     what is deployed, and what is pending. Update it at the end of
     every session.

  8. Session index:
     File: ODIN/System/SESSION_INDEX.txt
     One line per session. Update it when a session closes.
