MCTP Scoring — Your #1 Job

Every seller call gets a score. This tells Brock how motivated the seller is and whether to pursue the deal. Score every call, every time.

Letter What It Measures Max Points How to Score High
M — Motivation Why are they selling? 3 Divorce/death/foreclosure = 3. Downsizing = 1-2. "Just testing" = 0.
C — Condition Property condition 2 Needs major work = 2. Minor repairs = 1. Move-in ready = 0.
T — Timeline How fast do they need out? 3 30 days or less = 3. 60-90 days = 2. "No rush" = 0-1.
P — Price Are they realistic on price? 2 Open to wholesale offer = 2. Slightly high but flexible = 1. Way overpriced = 0.
🔥 HOT (8-10) — Brock gets alerted immediately. Priority deal.
⚡ WARM (5-7) — Brock gets alerted. Follow-up scheduled.
🧊 COLD (0-4) — Logged. ODIN may follow up automatically later.

Logging a Call — The mctp Command

After every seller call, log it in Slack like this:

Format mctp [Seller First Name] [Property Address] M:[0-3] C:[0-2] T:[0-3] P:[0-2] notes:[your notes]
Real Example — Hot Lead mctp John 4314 Leatherwood Ave Memphis M:3 C:2 T:3 P:1 notes:Divorce, needs out in 30 days, house needs full rehab, wants $80k but flexible
Real Example — Cold Lead mctp Sarah 892 Oak Street Memphis M:1 C:1 T:1 P:0 notes:Just curious, not motivated, wants full retail price
What happens after you log: ODIN calculates the score automatically, logs it to the database, and sends you a confirmation. If it's Hot or Warm, Brock gets an alert immediately in Slack AND Discord with your notes. ODIN may also run deal math automatically (ARV, what we can offer, potential profit).

XLeads — Our CRM

XLeads (powered by GoHighLevel) is where all our seller contacts live. ODIN is connected to it — every command you run through Slack can trigger actions in XLeads. You don't need to log into XLeads directly. ODIN handles it.

What XLeads stores: Every seller we've ever texted or called. Their phone, address, conversation history, and tags (Hot, Warm, Cold, Do-Not-Contact, etc.).
What ODIN does with it: Auto-tags sellers based on your MCTP score. Logs your conversation notes. Routes hot leads to Brock. Removes opt-outs automatically.

When Sellers Text Back

Sellers are already in our XLeads system from blast campaigns. When one texts back:

1

Do's and Don'ts

✅ DO

  • Log every call with the mctp command
  • Include notes — the more detail the better
  • Use script before calling a seller
  • Use lookup to verify owner before calling
  • Score honestly — don't inflate scores
  • Check leads daily for your pipeline

❌ DON'T

  • Skip logging a call — every call matters
  • Promise sellers a price — log and let Brock decide
  • Contact a seller marked Do-Not-Contact
  • Try to access other users' leads
  • Share your login credentials
  • Make any changes in XLeads directly (use ODIN)
