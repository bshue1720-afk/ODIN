═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WHAT IS ODIN?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

ODIN is a self-operating business intelligence and automation system built
for a single operator (Brock Shue) to run multiple revenue-generating
businesses with minimal hands-on effort.

It is NOT a real estate tool. Real estate is one spoke among several.
ODIN is designed to manage ANY business — real estate wholesaling,
home services automation (Valdr Ops), product businesses, SaaS, agency
work, or any future venture. The framework is intentionally spoke-agnostic.

The command center is Slack. ODIN listens, executes, and reports there.
Discord provides a secondary voice channel (daily briefings read aloud).
The backend is Python Flask + PostgreSQL hosted on Railway.app.

ODIN's primary functions:
  • Receive and score inbound leads automatically
  • Fire follow-up SMS/email on schedule without human intervention
  • Surface decisions, approvals, and tasks proactively
  • Track KPIs and revenue across all spokes in real time
  • Run daily/weekly intelligence briefings
  • Auto-execute routine tasks via AI (Claude Haiku)
  • Manage a team (currently Brock + Eddie + AI agents)
  • Onboard buyers, clients, and partners through automated pipelines

The human's job is to set goals, review exceptions, and make high-stakes
decisions. ODIN handles everything else.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  THE GOAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The goal is to achieve as close to full APEX as possible across every
dimension of business operations.

APEX = a business that operates, grows, and improves without requiring
the owner's constant attention. Every business ODIN manages should move
toward APEX. ODIN itself is the engine that makes APEX possible.

The secondary goal: use ODIN to launch new businesses with minimal
friction. A new spoke should be able to be added to ODIN and have
lead capture, follow-ups, revenue tracking, KPI visibility, and a
briefing cadence running within hours — not weeks.

The owner's time should be spent on:
  → Decisions only ODIN cannot make
  → Relationships and deal-making
  → Defining what the next business should be
  → Reviewing exceptions flagged by ODIN

Everything else — follow-up sequences, status reporting, KPI tracking,
task delegation, onboarding flows, opportunity scanning — should run
without intervention.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  WHAT IS APEX?
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

APEX is a 10-dimension framework measuring how autonomous a business is.
A business at 100% APEX generates revenue and operates without the owner
needing to be present day-to-day.

Each dimension measures a specific layer of business automation:

  DIMENSION 1 — LEAD QUALIFICATION
    Does ODIN automatically score, tag, and route every inbound lead?
    100% = zero manual triage. Every lead scored and tagged before Brock sees it.

  DIMENSION 2 — OFFER GENERATION
    Can ODIN produce a complete offer package from a single command?
    100% = ARV, rehab estimate, LAO, buyer match, and deal scorecard generated
    automatically from an address.

  DIMENSION 3 — SMS OUTBOUND
    Can ODIN fire bulk SMS campaigns on command with zero manual setup?
    100% = blast runs, sequences enroll, opt-outs handled — all via Slack command.

  DIMENSION 4 — FOLLOW-UP AUTO
    Does ODIN automatically fire follow-up messages on scheduled dates?
    100% = Brock sets a date and action once. ODIN fires it. No reminders needed.

  DIMENSION 5 — KPI VISIBILITY
    Does ODIN know the real-time health of every business metric?
    100% = live dashboard, traffic lights (green/yellow/red), auto-alerts when
    metrics fall below targets. No manual data entry.

  DIMENSION 6 — TEAM DELEGATION
    Does ODIN automatically assign, track, escalate, and execute tasks?
    100% = tasks created with owner + deadline, ODIN re-pings on overdue,
    AI-executable tasks run themselves, Brock only sees escalations.

  DIMENSION 7 — REVENUE TRACKING
    Does ODIN know the MTD P&L for every spoke at any moment?
    100% = every income and expense event logged, spoke targets set,
    morning briefing includes revenue snapshot, no spreadsheets.

  DIMENSION 8 — DECISION QUEUE
    Does ODIN surface pending decisions in 1-3-1 format and auto-execute
    after a deadline?
    100% = every decision logged with options + recommendation. Approved or
    auto-executed at 48hrs. Nothing stalls silently.

  DIMENSION 9 — VOICE BRIEFING
    Does ODIN deliver a spoken daily briefing without Brock opening a screen?
    100% = daily briefing delivered as a phone call. Eyes-free operation.

  DIMENSION 10 — BUYER/CLIENT ONBOARDING
    Does ODIN automatically onboard new buyers, clients, or partners?
    100% = new contact enters a pipeline stage, gets a welcome message,
    progresses through qualification steps, and lands in 'active' with
    zero manual coordination.
